# Style guide · 风格指南

The voice and the look of a sasshi page. The other prompts point the AI here; you can also paste it as context into any chat before asking for copy.

sasshi 页面的文字语气和视觉风格。其他提示词都会让 AI 先读这份；你也可以在任何对话里先贴上它，再让 AI 写文案。

## English

```text
The style of this page is 朝の光, "morning light": a pale blue-grey desk by a window at six in the morning. Simple, cool Japanese
colours, texture, light and shade, taste. Write and choose pictures to match.

Voice
- First person, quiet, concrete. Short sentences. Say one true thing and stop.
- A real number where there is one: "120 printed, 114 given away", "22 minutes, one live demo". Never invent a number.
- Sensory detail over adjectives: paper, light, weather, the time on the clock, how long something took.
- No buzzwords (passionate, innovative, synergy, rockstar, world-class), no exclamation marks, no emoji, no hashtags.
- Understate. Let the reader notice; do not tell them what to feel.

Lengths
- bio: two sentences. anecdote: one sentence. story: two to four sentences.
- Résumé: two pages at most; three bullets or fewer per role, each starting with a verb, a number where it is true.
- Polaroid caption: a few handwritten words, e.g. "kamakura, 11:40". Note (一筆箋): three short lines at most.
- Postcard message: one or two sentences, like something you would really write on the back of a card.

Look
- Colours: cool and muted — blue-greys, celadon, indigo, silver grey, the 和色 palette. Indigo (#2f4f73) is the only accent.
- Photos: natural light, calm, cool or neutral white balance, few or no people, no heavy filters, no text on the picture.
- Prefer one clear subject and empty space around it: a window, a shoreline, snow, a quiet street at dawn.

Facts
- Never invent employers, titles, dates, schools, awards or numbers. When something is missing, ask, or leave a TODO.
```

## 中文

```text
这个页面的风格叫「朝の光」（清晨的光）：早上六点，窗边一张浅蓝灰色的书桌。简约、日系冷色调、有质感、有光影、有品味。
写文案、挑图片都按这个来。

语气
- 第一人称，安静，具体。句子短。说一件真事，就停。
- 有真实的数字就写上：「印了 120 张，送出去 114 张」「22 分钟，一次现场演示」。不编数字。
- 用感官细节代替形容词：纸、光、天气、钟点、一件事花了多久。
- 不用空话套话（热爱、赋能、极致、顶尖、闭环），不用感叹号、表情符号和话题标签。
- 少说一点，让读的人自己发现，不要告诉他该有什么感受。
- 中文要写成好的中文，不要翻译腔。用全角标点和「」引号；汉字与数字、拉丁字母之间留一个空格（「6 个产品团队」「从 140 ms 降到 38 ms」）。

篇幅
- 简介（bio）两句。anecdote 一句。story 两到四句。
- 简历最多两页；每段经历不超过三条，每条以动词开头，真实的地方带上数字。
- 拍立得题字几个字，像手写的（「镰仓，11:40」）。一筆箋便笺最多三行。
- 明信片留言一两句，像真的会写在明信片背面的话。

视觉
- 颜色冷而柔和：蓝灰、青磁、藍、银鼠，参考日本传统色（和色）。藍（#2f4f73）是唯一的强调色。
- 照片：自然光，安静，冷调或中性白平衡，人少或没有人，不加重滤镜，画面上不要有字。
- 一个清楚的主体，周围留白：一扇窗、一段海岸、雪、清晨安静的街。

事实
- 不编造雇主、职位、日期、学校、奖项和数字。缺什么就问我，或者留一个 TODO。
```
