# Prompts

English · [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md)

Ready-made prompts for working on this project with an AI. Open the repository in an AI coding agent (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Copilot …), copy a prompt, fill in the angle brackets and send it. Every file has the prompt in English and in Chinese.

## Use the template

Make the page yours: your words, your pictures, your languages.

| Prompt | What it does |
|---|---|
| [make-it-yours](use/make-it-yours.md) | Everything in one go: your profile, résumé, pouch, albums, photos and (optionally) Chinese |
| [profile](use/profile.md) | Your name, bio and links, the résumé printed in the folio, the business card |
| [pouch](use/pouch.md) | The ticket, the polaroid and the note — asks you a few questions first |
| [albums](use/albums.md) | The card album and the postcards |
| [photos](use/photos.md) | Crops, sizes and the cool toning that fits the room's light; credits |
| [translate](use/translate.md) | A Simplified Chinese edition (`profile/zh.ts`) as careful as the English |
| [style](use/style.md) | The voice and the look; paste it as context into any chat before asking for copy |

## Build or extend it

How the project itself was made — the direction, the engines, the light, the sound — written as prompts you can use to build something like it, or to change it here.

| Prompt | What it does |
|---|---|
| [art-direction](build/art-direction.md) | Sets the tone: a room, a time of day and one light, then colour, type, layout, controls and motion |
| [ring-binder](build/ring-binder.md) | The 3D album: sleeve pages as physical chains, the case, the frosted cover, on-demand rendering |
| [clear-pouch](build/clear-pouch.md) | The zip pouch: 2D rigid bodies driven by the folder's motion, grabbing and flinging, printed objects |
| [paper](build/paper.md) | The résumé typeset onto A4 and turned like paper, the folding business card, the wheel zoom |
| [room-and-light](build/room-and-light.md) | The morning room under the canvas and the window's shadow cast for real in the 3D scene |
| [sound](build/sound.md) | Generated foley and the quiet ambience, picked by ear and levelled by measurement |
| [new-booklet](build/new-booklet.md) | A fourth booklet on the shelf, as a preset rather than a new engine |
| [review](build/review.md) | The quality bar a change has to meet before it ships |

The prompts never let the AI invent facts about you: they tell it to ask, or to leave a TODO.
